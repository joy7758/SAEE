# Conservative Novelty and Related-work Positioning

Status: `local_positioning_support_only`

## Recommended one-paragraph positioning

SAEE should be presented here as a local observational characterization of one
frozen reflexive evolutionary software object. It is not a replacement for
Tierra or Avida, not a new general theory of evolution, not a solver-superiority
claim, and not evidence of open-ended evolution. Relative to established
digital-evolution platforms and measurement toolkits, the narrow contribution
is a repository-linked protocol that maps a frozen runtime's identity-bounded
feedback, lineage DAG, categorical regime labels, and local evidence compression
to explicit, inspectable artifacts. The value of the LBA is therefore
diagnostic and methodological: it exposes what was observed, how the labels were
computed, and where the inference boundary ends.

## Comparison matrix

| Prior work | What it establishes | What lb120 does not claim | Narrow relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tierra | Seminal digital organisms evolving in a resource-constrained virtual machine | SAEE is not claimed to reproduce or surpass Tierra's ecological phenomena | Tierra is historical context for digital evolution |
| Avida | A controlled experimental platform for self-replicating digital organisms with extensive measurement and analysis tools | SAEE is not claimed to offer equivalent experimental breadth or validation | Avida sets the comparison bar for protocol clarity and analysis tooling |
| MODES Toolbox | Cross-system metrics for hallmarks of open-ended dynamics | lb120 does not report MODES metrics or open-ended evolution | MODES motivates explicit measurement definitions and the non-claim boundary |
| Ancestry-based analyses | Lineage and phylogeny metrics and visualizations for evolutionary dynamics | The reported 808 nodes and 1590 edges are not phylogenetic richness or divergence metrics | This work motivates precise lineage accounting |
| EC reproducibility guidance | Distinguishes reproducibility dimensions and emphasizes code, data, factors, and setup | Current artifacts do not recover every original execution detail | It motivates explicit hashes, paths, and declared unknowns |

## Venue fit

The official ALIFE 2026 topics include predictive methods for complex adaptive
and lifelike systems, self-* systems, constructive dynamical systems and
complexity, simulation and visualization tools, and reflective agents. lb120
fits only in a bounded sense: it reports a controlled local observation surface
for a reflective evolutionary software object. It should not be framed as
biology, external-world autonomy, or a production system.
