# ALIFE 2026 Late-Breaking Abstract Format Notes

Status: current local submission-prep notes checked on 2026-07-02.

## Venue Facts Used

- ALIFE 2026 takes place in Waterloo, Canada, and online from 17-21 August
  2026.
- Full Papers & Summaries submission is closed.
- Late-Breaking Abstracts are due 20 July 2026.
- Late-Breaking Abstracts are limited to a maximum of two pages, excluding
  references.
- Late-Breaking Abstracts may report new ideas and work in progress.
- Accepted Late-Breaking Abstracts are not included in proceedings and are
  presented as posters.
- Submissions are not anonymous; author names, affiliations, and funding
  information should be included.
- ALIFE 2026 uses single-blind review.
- The LBA sample form states that any AI use must be disclosed and described
  in acknowledgments.

## Local Policy

`paper_alife_lba/main.tex` is a compact local LaTeX proof source. It should be
treated as a submission-prep artifact, not proof of official-template
compliance. Before upload, the author must confirm whether the portal requires
the official template source or accepts the generated PDF, and must replace the
temporary funding sentence with final funding information or an explicit
no-funding statement.

## Claim Boundary

The late-breaking abstract is a compression of the frozen SAEE paper-facing
object. It introduces no new theory, experiments, kernels, runtime behavior,
candidate laws, publication claims, or validation claims.
