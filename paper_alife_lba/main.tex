\begin{abstract}
This late-breaking abstract presents SAEE as a frozen Local Canonical
Reflexive Evolutionary Dynamical System Object (LCR-REDS). The contribution is
not a new evolutionary algorithm or a universal theory of evolution. It is a
local scientific-object characterization of an artificial-life-style system in
which population state, lineage structure, mutable operators, and observer
state are represented together under identity constraints. Existing local
simulation surfaces show a single dominant stable regime, a stable lineage
basin, bounded semantic drift, and preserved lineage integrity in the frozen
configuration. These observations are reported as local candidate regularities
only; they are not externally validated laws, benchmark superiority claims, or
evidence of open-ended evolution.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Motivation.}
Artificial life systems often study emergence, population dynamics, and
lineage behavior through executable simulations. SAEE focuses on a narrower
question: how to describe a completed local evolutionary object when its
selection and transformation operators are part of the observed dynamical
surface rather than fixed background machinery. This framing is intended for
ALife-style interpretation of reflexive evolutionary dynamics, not for
production deployment or general optimization.

\textbf{Model.}
The frozen object is summarized as
\[
  SAEE = (\Omega, G, T, S, L, R, \mu),
  \qquad
  P_{t+1}=S_{\theta_t}(T_{\phi_t}(P_t)).
\]
Here $\Omega$ is evolution space, $G$ is genome space, $T$ is a transformation
operator, $S$ is a selection operator or field, $L$ is lineage structure, $R$
is reflexive observer state, and $\mu$ is the population measure. The
operator parameters are treated as state-coupled:
\[
  \theta_t=g(P_t,R_t,L_t),\qquad
  \phi_t=h(P_t,R_t,L_t),\qquad
  R_{t+1}=\Psi(R_t,P_t,L_t).
\]
Admissible transitions remain bounded by an identity constraint
$I(X_t,X_{t+1})\geq\theta_I$. The paper-facing theory is compressed into three
local laws: reflexive coupled evolution, dynamic selection topology, and
bounded identity drift. In this late-breaking version, those laws are treated
as descriptive constraints over the frozen object, not as universal claims.

\textbf{Observed local behavior.}
The current evidence surface is derived from existing repository logs and
reports only. It does not add new experiments. The reported long-horizon
surface records 100 generations, a dominant \texttt{stable\_regime}, a
dominant \texttt{stable\_lineage\_basin}, no observed collapse, no observed
cross-regime transition, and a lineage graph surface with 808 nodes and 1590
edges. A separate Phase II drift surface reports bounded semantic drift with
observed maximum 0.2 under the frozen constraints.

\textbf{Interpretation and boundary.}
SAEE is positioned as a local canonical scientific object for studying
reflexive evolutionary dynamical systems with mutable operators,
lineage-coupled feedback, and identity constraints. The current result is
best read as an attractor-dominated local case study: the system stabilizes
into a strongly convergent phase-space region instead of demonstrating
unbounded open-endedness. The five extracted regularities remain candidate
regularities of this object only. This abstract makes no claim of universal
evolutionary law, external validation, physical-law status, benchmark
superiority, manuscript acceptance, publication, release, DOI assignment, or
portal submission.
