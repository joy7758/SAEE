## Scope and article-type evidence

The official guidelines describe Artificial Life as covering original
scientific and engineering research involving synthesis of life and life-like
phenomena in software, hardware, and wetware. They list `Letter` as a short
communication reporting original research, typically below 2,000 words.

The SAEE manuscript reports a local digital-evolution population, lineage, and
bounded-dynamics result. It is submitted as a Letter, not as a universal theory
or open-ended-evolution claim.

## Cost evidence

The official guidelines describe open access as an option available after
acceptance and link to an associated fee. MIT Press separately lists
Artificial Life among its hybrid open-access journals. Therefore the selected
standard subscription route does not require the optional OA fee.

No submission fee, mandatory APC, mandatory page charge, mandatory color fee,
or conference registration is selected or authorized. If the submission
portal later presents any mandatory author payment, the workflow must stop and
the venue gate changes to `FAIL`.

## Duplicate-publication boundary

ALIFE 2026 `lb120` was a one-page Late-Breaking Abstract accepted for a poster
route. It was not included in proceedings and has no publication or DOI claim.
The author abandoned participation before registration. The cover letter
discloses that history; the journal Letter is not concurrently under review.
