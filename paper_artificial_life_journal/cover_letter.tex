\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please consider the enclosed Letter, ``Bounded Evolution in a Reflexive
Digital Population: A Local SAEE Characterization,'' for publication in
\textit{Artificial Life}.

The manuscript fits the journal's software artificial-life scope by examining
the evolutionary dynamics and lineage structure of a constructed digital
population. Its contribution is deliberately bounded: it reports when a system
designed around reflexive evolutionary concepts exhibits convergent rather
than open-ended behavior. The analysis separates a 100-generation fixed-kernel
record from a six-generation reflexive-analysis record and translates internal
labels into operational measurements. This produces a falsifiable local
baseline without claiming mathematical attractor existence, universal laws,
external validation, or benchmark superiority.

based on an earlier and much less detailed account, was accepted for ALIFE 2026
as submission \texttt{lb120}. It is not included in conference proceedings,
has no DOI, and will not be presented because the corresponding author withdrew

complete operational definitions, provenance boundaries, quantitative traces,

the result.
