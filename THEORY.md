# SAEE Theory Baseline
# SAEE 理论基线

SAEE means Silicon-Amplified Evolutionary Ecology.
SAEE 指 Silicon-Amplified Evolutionary Ecology（硅基放大演化生态）。

Digital Biosphere（数字生物圈） is not a biological imitation; it is a Silicon-Amplified Evolutionary Ecology（硅基放大演化生态）.

The project treats agent populations, repositories, documents, experiments, and product prototypes as evolving phenotypes（表型） that can inherit, vary, compete, sleep, roll back, and leave verifiable lineage.
本项目把智能体种群、仓库、文档、实验和产品原型视为可继承、可变异、可竞争、可休眠、可回滚、可留下可验证谱系的 phenotype（表型）。

Carbon-based evolution contributes the organizing principles: heredity, variation, selection, niche pressure, lineage, adaptation, and extinction.
碳基演化提供组织原则：遗传、变异、选择、生态位压力、谱系、适应和灭绝。

Silicon-based systems amplify those principles through cheap branching, parallel simulation, global memory, snapshots, rollback, programmable evaluation, explicit permission boundaries, and durable archives.
硅基系统通过低成本分叉、并行模拟、全局记忆、快照、回滚、程序化评估、显式权限边界和持久档案放大这些原则。

The core is evolution.
核心是进化。

Audit（审计）, evidence（证据）, governance（治理）, sandbox（沙盒）, and rollback（回滚） are immune and evidence infrastructure. They support selection, trust, and recovery, but they are not the project identity.
审计、证据、治理、沙盒和回滚是免疫与证据基础设施。它们支持选择、信任和恢复，但不是项目身份。

The originality boundary is not "AI agents that audit themselves" and not "digital life imitation." The boundary is a programmable ecological engine that can sense, extract traits, branch genomes, develop variants, evaluate Pareto fitness, preserve lineage, and safely reuse failure.
原创边界不是“自审计 AI 智能体”，也不是“数字生命仿制”。原创边界是一个可编程生态引擎：它能够感知、提取性状、分叉基因型、发育变体、评估帕累托适应度、保存谱系，并安全复用失败。
