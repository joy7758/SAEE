# SAEE MVP Pricing and Packaging

Status: internal packaging plan, not a published pricing page and not a sales
offer.

## Commercial Packaging

### Free Tier

Purpose:

- adoption;
- demos;
- academic exploration;
- design-partner lead generation.

Limits:

- one agent or strategy test;
- limited runs;
- no export;
- synthetic scenarios only.

Boundary:

- not production;
- not private deployment;
- no customer secrets.

### Pro Team

Target price band:

```text
99-499 USD / month
```

Included:

- multi-agent testing;
- full reports;
- export;
- scenario packs;
- retained experiment history.

Customer-facing units:

- scenario runs;
- evaluated episodes;
- saved reports;
- seats;
- retention window.

### Enterprise

Target price band:

```text
20k-200k USD / year
```

Included:

- private deployment;
- custom scenarios;
- internal model testing;
- audit logs;
- onboarding;
- support.

Commercial services:

- paid pilot;
- scenario-pack setup;
- private deployment review;
- workflow integration support.

## Sales Message

Primary message:

```text
We test which AI agents survive long-term competition.
```

Do not lead with:

- evolution engine;
- simulation platform;
- research system;
- phase-space science.
