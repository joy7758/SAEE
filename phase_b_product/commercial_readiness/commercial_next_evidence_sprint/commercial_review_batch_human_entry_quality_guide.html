QFRB-001

assigned_human_owner

合格写法：role, team, or person reference explicitly approved by a human

质量规则：Must name who owns the support-contact decision or the approved internal owner reference.

拒绝写法：Reject blank-looking placeholders, guessed owners, or direct personal phone/email not meant for public config.

占位示例：EXAMPLE_ONLY: support owner role reviewed by human

隐私提醒：Prefer internal role or approved owner reference; avoid raw personal contact details.

QFRB-002

owner_contact_reference

合格写法：internal ticket, meeting note, document path, or approval reference

质量规则：Must point to a human-reviewable internal source for the owner/contact decision.

拒绝写法：Reject unsupported personal contact data, vague memory, or unapproved public contact claims.

占位示例：EXAMPLE_ONLY: internal approval record reference

隐私提醒：Use a reference ID or internal document name instead of private phone/email.

QFRB-003

target_review_date

合格写法：YYYY-MM-DD or explicit reviewed date reference

质量规则：Must be a concrete date or an explicit reviewed date source.

拒绝写法：Reject vague dates such as soon, later, next week, or guessed current date.

占位示例：EXAMPLE_ONLY: YYYY-MM-DD

隐私提醒：Date only; no personal details required.

QFRB-004

owner_acknowledged_scope

合格写法：true/false/hold plus reviewer reference

质量规则：Must say whether the owner acknowledged scope, and point to the human review source.

拒绝写法：Reject unsupported approved claims, production-ready claims, or customer-facing launch claims.

占位示例：EXAMPLE_ONLY: hold - scope not yet approved by owner

隐私提醒：Reference the review record; do not expose private discussion details.

QFRB-005

human_approval_reference

合格写法：approval record, meeting note, ticket, or signed-off internal reference

质量规则：Must identify the human approval evidence source without claiming execution.

拒绝写法：Reject verbal-only claims with no reference, or claims that approval equals launch.

占位示例：EXAMPLE_ONLY: approval record ID

隐私提醒：Use record identifiers; avoid copying private messages.

QFRB-006

human_reviewer_name

合格写法：reviewer role, team, person name, or internal reviewer reference

质量规则：Must identify who reviewed the entry at a human-readable level.

拒绝写法：Reject anonymous approval, guessed names, or unapproved personal contact details.

占位示例：EXAMPLE_ONLY: reviewer role or internal reviewer reference

隐私提醒：Use role/reference if public names are not approved for config.

QFRB-007

review_date

合格写法：YYYY-MM-DD or explicit reviewed date reference

质量规则：Must be the actual human review date or a traceable date reference.

拒绝写法：Reject vague dates such as today, soon, later, or guessed current date.

占位示例：EXAMPLE_ONLY: YYYY-MM-DD

隐私提醒：Date only; no personal details required.

QFRB-008

selected_support_contact_channel

合格写法：approved channel type or internal reference, such as support_email_candidate, ticketing_system_candidate, or hold_no_channel_approved

质量规则：Must describe the selected support-contact channel type without pretending it is live.

拒绝写法：Reject live public contact claims, production support claims, or customer-ready language if not separately approved.

占位示例：EXAMPLE_ONLY: hold_no_channel_approved

隐私提醒：Do not expose real addresses or forms until the channel is approved.

QFRB-009

decision_summary

合格写法：concise human decision summary with boundary-safe wording

质量规则：Must summarize the human decision while preserving hold/not-production-ready boundaries.

拒绝写法：Reject production-ready, customer-validated, launched, or universal support claims.

占位示例：EXAMPLE_ONLY: support channel decision remains on hold pending owner approval

隐私提醒：Summarize the decision, not private deliberation.

QFRB-010

abuse_handling_path_defined

合格写法：true/false/hold plus evidence reference

质量规则：Must state whether abuse handling is defined and cite the human-review source.

拒绝写法：Reject unsupported live support, incident response, production-ready, or customer-support claims.

占位示例：EXAMPLE_ONLY: hold - abuse handling path not yet approved

隐私提醒：Reference the process record; do not expose private escalation contacts.
