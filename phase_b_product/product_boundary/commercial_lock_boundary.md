Status: private asset boundary, not a release event.

## Commercial Lock Mode

SAEE has entered a commercial lock mode after the Zenodo definition-only
publication.

Commercial lock means:

- the scientific definition can be cited;
- the product direction can be described;
- the private core remains closed;
- customer-facing value is expressed through reports, workflows, and private
  deployment options, not through kernel disclosure.
