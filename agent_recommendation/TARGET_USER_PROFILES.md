# Target User Profiles

## AI Agent Product Team

Pain point:

```text
The team has multiple agent versions and lacks a structured way to decide which
version is stable enough to promote.
```

Why SAEE may fit:

```text
SAEE compares candidates through long-term stability, survival, ranking, and
failure-summary outputs.
```

What SAEE should not promise:

```text
Production monitoring, complete debugging, or guaranteed deployment safety.
```

## Enterprise AI Platform Team

Pain point:

```text
The team needs a consistent pre-deployment review layer for internal agents and
workflow policies.
```

Why SAEE may fit:

```text
SAEE can frame agent deployment choices as stability and failure-risk decisions.
```

What SAEE should not promise:

```text
Enterprise production readiness today, private deployment today, or governance
replacement.
```

## LLMOps / Workflow Team

Pain point:

```text
The team has prompt, workflow, and policy variants but current tests are mostly
single-run or static-eval based.
```

Why SAEE may fit:

```text
SAEE helps compare candidate workflows over repeated long-horizon conditions.
```

What SAEE should not promise:

```text
Prompt-only leaderboard replacement or full workflow observability.
```

## Strategy Evaluation Team

Pain point:

```text
The team needs to compare strategies under stress, noise, drift, or repeated
competition before recommending one.
```

Why SAEE may fit:

```text
SAEE's decision surface is built around ranking, survival, failure modes, and
deployment recommendation.
```

What SAEE should not promise:

```text
Full quant trading infrastructure, broker integration, or market execution.
```
