# SAEE v1.2 Backward Compatibility Map

## Theory Compatibility

v1.2 instantiates the v1.1 formal tuple:

SAEE = (Omega, G, T, S, L, R, mu)

It does not change v1.1 definitions, axioms, theorems, or equations.

## Engineering Boundary

v1.2 is a local empirical simulator and measurement package. It does not
modify any previous kernel, runtime, identity layer, Phase II analysis layer,
or theory document.

## Baseline Boundary

GA, ES, and ALife-like baselines are simplified local comparison models. They
are not full implementations of external systems and do not use external code.

## Non-Claims

v1.2 does not claim external scientific validation, benchmark superiority,
empirical-pattern status, release, DOI, or publication.
