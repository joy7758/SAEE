**Figure 1. Synthetic parasitic phase transition under governance control in a
Digital Biosphere Instance.** The figure shows three trajectories from the
SAEE v1.2 `parasitic_phase` system. Panel 1 plots the bounded parasitic index
`Phi(t)` for no governance (A), weak governance (B), and strong governance (C),
with the critical boundary `Phi_c = 0.60` shown as a dashed line. A crosses the
boundary rapidly, B crosses later, and C remains below the boundary in the
current closure run, demonstrating a governance-dependent shift in phase
transition timing. Panel 2 shows entropy dynamics, where transition-associated
loss or fluctuation of diversity is visible as the system moves away from a
distributed regime. Panel 3 shows dominance emergence, the order-like variable
corresponding to lineage/resource takeover. Together, the panels show that
`Phi` functions as an empirical order parameter for the implemented synthetic
multi-agent DBI: it rises with reward drift, resource concentration, and
dominance, and it responds to governance strength. The result is local and
synthetic; it does not claim external real-world validation or broad behavior
across all multi-agent systems.
