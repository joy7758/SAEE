## Truth Boundary

The manuscript proposes a research category and a research agenda. It does not
claim that the category is recognized, that a complete trust infrastructure has
been implemented, or that SAEE currently provides trust-continuity evaluation.
SAEE is treated as the originating research program and as an example of the
evidence-evaluation boundary, not as a validated product proof.

The package meets the current technical preparation checks and includes the
journal's accepted editable source format. No submission is authorized. The
human author must still review the final language and competing-interest
disclosure and explicitly authorize any portal action.
