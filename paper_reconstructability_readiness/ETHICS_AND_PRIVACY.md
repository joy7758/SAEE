# Ethics and privacy statement

## Study inputs

```text
synthetic_json_only=true
human_participants=false
human_intervention=false
personal_or_health_records=false
customer_data=false
live_agent_interaction=false
external_identity_lookup=false
network_access=false
```

The study operates only on authored synthetic JSON packages. It does not
recruit, observe, intervene on, or make decisions about people. It does not use
health records, behavior logs, customer records, private messages, production
telemetry, or externally verified identities. On this design, research ethics
approval and informed consent are not applicable.

Author names, affiliations, e-mails, and ORCIDs are publication-administration
metadata, not experimental observations. Private author profiles, dates of
birth, phone numbers, residential addresses, photographs, biographies, and any
source-document credentials are excluded from the experiment and
supplementary artifact.

This local applicability statement is not a legal determination for a future
study. Any follow-up involving real people, personal data, live agents,
customers, or consequential external decisions requires a new ethics, privacy,
authorization, and data-governance review.
