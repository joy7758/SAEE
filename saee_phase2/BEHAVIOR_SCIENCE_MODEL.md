# Behavior Science Model

## Purpose

Phase II treats SAEE as an observed evolutionary behavior field. It measures
what the local system does across generations instead of changing how the
system evolves.

## Measurement Surfaces

- Behavior trajectory: population size, active/dormant split, identity score,
  mutation counts, semantic stabilization, semantic drift, and bounded observer
  status.
- Attractor map: recurring state signatures and convergence basins.
- Regime log: stable, exploratory, chaotic, and collapse regime labels.
- Topology map: nodes, edges, branching density, bottlenecks, and diversity
  growth or decay.
- Drift model: structural drift, semantic drift, and behavioral drift.
- Empirical laws: local rules extracted from observed behavior.

## Scientific Boundary

The laws are empirical local observations. They are not universal formal laws,
not externally validated, and not production science claims.
