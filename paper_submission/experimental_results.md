## Observational Setup

The reported results come from a local-only, passive long-horizon observation.
No new experiment is generated by this paper package.

## Interpretation

The results support bounded convergence:

- population remains stable;
- collapse is not observed;
- lineage integrity is preserved;
- variance shows a converging tendency;
- structural growth remains bounded by a stable lineage DAG.

The results do not support an open-ended evolution claim under current
constraints.
