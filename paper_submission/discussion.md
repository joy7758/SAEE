# Discussion

SAEE shows a clear convergence pattern under current constraints. This matters
because computational evolutionary systems are often discussed through the lens
of open-endedness, while this object demonstrates a different empirical
profile: stable attractor dominance.

The dominant `stable_lineage_basin` suggests that the measured constraints
compress the phase space. The stable population and preserved lineage topology
show that evolution can continue locally without unbounded diversification.
The absence of collapse events and cross-regime transitions further supports a
strongly convergent interpretation.

This result should not be overread. The paper does not claim that SAEE proves a
universal law of artificial evolution. It does not claim external validation.
It does not claim open-ended behavior. It defines and reports a frozen
empirical phase space object.

The separation between public scientific object and private implementation is
deliberate. The paper publishes what SAEE is and what was observed, not how the
private implementation works.
