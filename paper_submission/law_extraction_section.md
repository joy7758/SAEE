# Law Extraction Section

## Method Boundary

Law extraction is performed over existing observations only. No new data is
generated and no runtime behavior is modified.

## Candidate Laws

1. Attractor Dominance
2. Regime Non-Transition
3. Lineage Stability
4. Bounded Diversity
5. Fitness Convergence

## Claim Status

```text
candidate_law_count: 5
external_validated_law_count: 0
universal_law_claim: false
```

## Interpretation

The candidate laws summarize local regularities in a frozen empirical object.
They are useful for falsification and future comparison, but they are not
presented as universal laws.

## Falsification Principle

Each candidate law can be downgraded or rejected if unchanged constrained
observations produce:

- additional dominant attractor basins;
- nonzero cross-regime transitions;
- lineage integrity failure;
- unbounded population diversification;
- repeated variance divergence.
