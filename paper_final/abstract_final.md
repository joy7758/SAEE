## Publication-Ready Abstract

We present SAEE as a Local Canonical Reflexive Evolutionary Dynamical System
Object (LCR-REDS Object): a bounded scientific object for studying reflexive
evolutionary dynamics with mutable operators, lineage-coupled feedback, and
identity constraints. SAEE is not framed as an optimizer, autonomous
intelligence system, universal theory of evolution, or externally validated
physical law. It is a local empirical phase-space object constructed from a
frozen runtime, observation traces, phase-space compression, a candidate law
set, and a canonical global state protocol.

Within the current frozen evidence boundary, SAEE behaves as a strongly
convergent evolutionary dynamical object rather than an open-ended
evolutionary system. The observed phase space is dominated by a single stable
lineage basin, with the only recorded transition being
`stable_regime -> stable_regime`. Collapse and cross-regime transitions are not
observed under the current constraints, while lineage integrity remains
preserved and population diversity stays bounded.
