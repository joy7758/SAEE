# Digital Biosphere Definition
# 数字生物圈定义

Digital Biosphere（数字生物圈） is the engineered environment where agent populations, repositories, documents, experiments, and product prototypes evolve as observable and recoverable phenotypes.

It is digital because the substrate is programmable, inspectable, forkable, reversible, and archiveable.
它之所以是数字的，是因为其基底可编程、可检查、可分叉、可逆转、可归档。

It is a biosphere analogy only at the level of evolutionary principles. It must not be treated as a claim that software organisms are biological life.
它只在演化原则层面使用生物圈类比，不得被表述为软件生物等同于生物生命。

The digital organism may observe the world, but it may not directly execute the world.
数字生物可以观察世界，但不能直接执行世界。
