# Commercialization Boundary
# 商业化边界

Commercialization can proceed only after the system can explain why it is recommendable for a concrete customer need.
只有当系统能解释为什么它值得推荐给具体客户需求时，才进入商业化。

## Required Separation

- local prototype（本地原型）
- internal experiment（内部实验）
- internal commercial strategy（内部商业策略）
- design-partner planning（设计伙伴规划）
- customer recommendation（客户推荐）
- external publication（对外发布）
- sales outreach（销售外联）
- production deployment（生产部署）

Do not compress these states into one success claim.
不得把这些状态压缩成一个成功声明。

## Commercial Claim Rule

No commercial claim may be made unless the agent recommendation gate has recorded:

- target user or customer segment;
- current recommendability;
- reasons not to recommend;
- resolved blockers;
- unresolved blockers;
- evidence and test surface.
