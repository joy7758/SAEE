## 中文策略

国内云厂商入口以中文解释为主，保留协议名、状态常量、路径和工具名称的英文原文。

## English technical summary

SAEE has local capability mappings, controlled research evidence and agent-readable entry packages. These assets are preparation surfaces only. They do not establish vendor endorsement, official integration, hosted availability, external adoption or production readiness.
