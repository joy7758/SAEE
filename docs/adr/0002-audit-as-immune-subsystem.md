# ADR-0002: Audit as Immune Subsystem
# ADR-0002：审计作为免疫子系统

## Status

Accepted（已接受）

## Decision

Audit（审计） exists to support selection, lineage, rollback, and external trust. It is not the product core.

Audit, evidence, governance, sandbox, and rollback are part of the immune governance plane.
审计、证据、治理、沙盒和回滚属于免疫治理平面。

## Consequences

- Audit features must explain which evolution decision they support.
- 审计功能必须说明它支持哪个演化决策。
- Evidence records must not be marketed as the whole project.
- 证据记录不得被包装成整个项目。
- Naming and documentation must keep SAEE and Digital Biosphere Evolution Engine as the mainline identity.
- 命名与文档必须保持 SAEE 和 Digital Biosphere Evolution Engine 作为主线身份。
