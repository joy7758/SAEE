\section{Model}

SAEE is represented as a frozen local scientific object:
\[
  SAEE = (\Omega, G, T, S, L, R, \mu).
\]
Here $\Omega$ is the evolution space, $G$ is genome space, $T$ is the
transformation operator, $S$ is the selection operator or field, $L$ is lineage
structure, $R$ is reflexive observer state, and $\mu$ is the population
measure.

The paper uses one compressed evolution equation:
\[
  P_{t+1} = S_{\theta_t}(T_{\phi_t}(P_t)).
\]
The parameters $\theta_t$ and $\phi_t$ are interpreted as state-dependent
selection and transformation parameters coupled to population, lineage, and
observer state:
\[
  \theta_t = g(P_t, R_t, L_t), \quad
  \phi_t = h(P_t, R_t, L_t).
\]
The observer state is updated as:
\[
  R_{t+1} = \Psi(R_t, P_t, L_t).
\]
Admissible transitions are bounded by the identity constraint:
\[
  I(X_t, X_{t+1}) \geq \theta_I.
\]

The formal surface is interpreted through three compressed laws only:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Reflexive Coupled Evolution Law.
  \item Dynamic Selection Topology Law.
  \item Bounded Identity Drift Law.
\end{enumerate}
These are part of the frozen theory compression. This ALife-format package
does not add laws or modify the formal theory.

\paragraph{Scope of analysis.}
All quantitative statements in this manuscript refer to the fixed SAEE kernel
and the reported local run configuration only. We do not claim cross-kernel
robustness, external validation, benchmark superiority, or universal
evolutionary laws.
