# ALife Format Notes

Status: local ALife-style formatting notes for SAEE, updated after hostile-review pass.

## Current Venue Evidence

Checked on 2026-07-02 against the public ALIFE 2026 call page:

- The official ALIFE 2026 call page exposes template links for Overleaf,
  LaTeX, and MS Word.
- Full papers are 3 to 8 pages long, not including references or
  acknowledgements.
- Accepted full papers are published by MIT Press as open-access conference
  proceedings.
- ALIFE 2026 submissions are not anonymous; author names, affiliations, and
  funding information should be included.
- ALIFE 2026 uses a single-blind review process.

## Local Formatting Policy

`main.tex` still uses a conservative local LaTeX `article` skeleton for
drafting. Before any real submission, replace the document class and front
matter with the official target-year ALIFE 2026 template. Do not change the
frozen SAEE claims while changing the presentation layer.
