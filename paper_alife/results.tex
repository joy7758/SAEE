\section{Results}

\paragraph{Interpretive scope.}
The statistics reported in this section are descriptive of the present frozen
object. They should not be read as evidence of open-ended evolution, broad
robustness, or external validity beyond the analyzed configuration.

The observed regime graph contains a single empirical transition:
\[
\texttt{stable\_regime} \rightarrow \texttt{stable\_regime}.
\]
The observed transition probability in the compressed phase graph is 1.0 for
this self-transition. No cross-regime transition is treated as an empirical
fact.
