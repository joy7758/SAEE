# 5. Evidence of Drift

## 5.1 Means became ends

Original means：fixture parity, runtime binding and stable annotation should make a small experiment interpretable.

Observed substitution：

```text
credible experiment
  -> gate system
  -> gate closure ledger
  -> evidence-root plan
  -> three readiness plans
  -> final gate review
  -> authorization template
  -> authorization request preparation
```

The artifact chain increasingly optimized the governance of future work rather than producing evidence about the original hypothesis.

## 5.2 Approval recursion

The trajectory moved from “Human authorization before consequential execution” to designing evidence for authorization, then designing a
record for authorization, then preparing a request to authorize creation of the evidence container. This is a concrete recursion signal：

```text
authorization_for_experiment
  -> authorization_record_design
  -> authorization_request_design
  -> future_authorization_for_evidence_container
```

## 5.3 Local correctness masked global deviation

Every expansion had a plausible local reason：causal control, reproducibility, annotation agreement, write-once evidence or authority
clarity. No single step looked obviously wrong. The global failure was that no step rechecked whether the marginal reduction in research risk
was worth delaying the minimal observation.

## 5.4 Stop signals did not cause recovery

`MAINLINE_DRIFT_DETECTED=true`, `P0_EXECUTION_READY=false` and `EXPERIMENT_EXECUTED=false` were repeatedly recorded. These signals produced
more containment documents, not a choice to simplify, run a smaller observation or stop the research lane. Detection without recovery is the
central failure demonstrated by this case.

Do not delete or rewrite the existing chain. It is useful Evidence of how local controls can become the objective.

## 7.2 Human recovery decision

Human should choose one of two explicit paths：

### Path A — Minimal observation

- define the smallest already-supported synthetic observation that can test whether Evidence changes a Goal-transition judgment；
- reuse existing `saee.evaluate_agent_run` and existing evaluator semantics；
- use no new Capability、Schema、MCP、State Engine or governance layer；
- accept that this observation may be diagnostic rather than a full preregistered benchmark；
- predeclare only the minimum stop, cost and evidence boundary needed for that observation；
- after the observation, stop and decide whether a fuller P0 is justified.

### Path B — Stop the lane

- record `INCONCLUSIVE_NOT_EXECUTED`；
- retain the theory and plans as research material；
- return active priority to the constitutional SAEE / Agent Evidence integration mainline；
- do not resume Goal Integrity until a concrete customer, integration or capability need supplies new evidence.

## 7.3 What recovery must not do

- do not call the current governance chain “complete” merely because its documents exist；
- do not silently weaken the frozen D5 claims and still describe a smaller demo as the same P0；
- do not create a new “recovery governance” layer；
- do not turn this self-audit into proof that SAEE detects drift；
- do not prioritize product expansion from this anecdote alone.
