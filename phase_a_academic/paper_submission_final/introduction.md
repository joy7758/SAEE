# Introduction

Computational evolutionary systems are often described by their mechanisms:
mutation, selection, reproduction, and lineage tracking. SAEE is framed here
from a different publication angle: as an empirical object whose long-horizon
behavior can be observed, compressed into phase-space structure, and used to
extract falsifiable local regularities.

The purpose of this paper package is to define SAEE in academic space without
publishing the implementation. The paper focuses on three observed properties:

- dominant attractor convergence;
- bounded population diversity;
- stable lineage topology.

This framing is intentionally conservative. The evidence supports a convergent
evolutionary dynamical object, not open-ended evolution.
