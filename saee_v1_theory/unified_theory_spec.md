# SAEE v1.0 Unified Theory Specification

## Name

SAEE v1.0 Theoretical Physics Layer

## Formal Object

SAEE_Theory = (G, M, S, L, E, I)

with expanded state:

X_t = (P_t, G, M_t, S_t, L_t, Q_t, O_t, I_t)

## Purpose

SAEE v1.0 defines evolution as a formal computational-physical system with
reflexive and identity-constrained dynamics.

It provides a universe of discourse for evolutionary systems, independent of
any particular medium or construction.

## Core Components

### Genome Space G

The abstract space of heritable state descriptions.

### Mutation Operator Field M

The abstract field of variation transformations.

### Selection Pressure Field S

The abstract field that assigns survival, dormancy, propagation, or extinction
tendencies.

### Lineage Topology L

The graph-theoretic or higher-order structure of descent and transformation.

### Evolution Dynamics E

The transition function over evolutionary states.

### Identity Constraint I

The continuity function that bounds transformation without requiring
unchanging state.

## Theoretical Claims

SAEE v1.0 asserts:

1. Evolution requires heritable state, variation, selection, and lineage.
2. Evolution is shaped by constraint, not merely by possibility.
3. Selection pressure can drift as populations and environments change.
4. Lineage topology has entropy and structural dynamics.
5. Observation can become causal in reflexive evolution.
6. Identity can remain stable under bounded transformation.
7. Evolutionary systems can undergo phase transitions.
8. New dimensions of evolution can emerge.

## Theory Validity Conditions

The theory is internally valid when:

- all primitive spaces are defined;
- state transitions are formally expressible;
- selection, lineage, reflexivity, and identity have explicit relations;
- phase transitions can be described as regime changes;
- no assumption requires a particular realization medium.

## Boundary Conditions

SAEE v1.0 does not define operational procedures, construction steps,
process control, distribution channels, or artifact formats.

It is a theory of evolutionary universes, not a recipe for building one.

## Minimal Research Program

A research program derived from SAEE v1.0 may study:

- formal properties of lineage entropy;
- stability conditions for reflexive coupling;
- identity continuity metrics;
- attractor classes in evolution space;
- phase transition thresholds;
- universality classes of constrained evolution;
- dimensional emergence in adaptive systems.
